\# WUA Food Delivery Requirements



\## Purpose

The WUA Food Delivery System allows university students and staff to order food from campus cafeterias.



\## Main Requirements

1\. Users should be able to register and log in.

2\. Users should be able to browse available food items and prices.

3\. Users should be able to place food orders.

4\. Users should be able to track their order status.

5\. The system should display clear information about food items and prices.
